HA Relapse 0.4.11 is upload-first.

Build: python3 build_bundle.py
Audit: python3 audit_bundle.py

The build writes dist/relapse-local-ha-0.4.11.zip and dist/SHA256SUMS.txt.
The ZIP includes README.md and the relapse_local app folder.
Follow README.md to install it.
Upload payloads in Home Assistant -> HA Relapse -> Open web UI.
Do not embed etaHEN, kstuff, ShadowMount, nanoDNS or other user payloads in the image.
Only the exploit's required ELF loader and kernel blob are packaged.

Keep README.md, BUNDLING.txt, build_bundle.py, audit_bundle.py, tests/, docs/,
relapse_local/ and .gitignore in source Git. Preserve upstream notices.
dist/, local backups, downloaded payloads and generated verification files
are Git-ignored. Attach the ZIP/checksum as release downloads if publishing.

Screenshots are in docs/screenshots/ and kept outside the install ZIP and
container image. Files named ps5-*-preview.jpg are local browser previews.
